THE MIRACLE OF CVSTODIA

"In the silent cloisters of the Silent Sorrow, the brotherhood lies slain... only the Penitent One remains..."

In the sacred land of Cvstodia, a mysterious force known as The Miracle has transformed the citizens' deepest guilt, faith, and remorse into terrifying physical forms and endless penance.

You wake as the Penitent One, the only survivor of the Brotherhood of the Silent Sorrow. Trapped in a curse of eternal rebirth, you must lift your legendary blade, Mea Culpa, and put an end to the nightmare.

Navigate the dark ruins, claim the Tears of Atonement, and search for the final truth behind the Curse.

CONTROLLING THE PENITENT ONE

D-PAD Move Left and Right. Press UP to climb ladders or cross doorways.
A BUTTON Jump up. Grip ledges to climb up high platforms.
B BUTTON Slash with Mea Culpa. Hold B to charge — release after a full charge for a devastating wide arc strike.
DOWN + B While on the ground: spend 25 Fervour to unleash the Lorquiana piercing wave. In mid-air: Downward Pogo Thrust.
SELECT Hold for over a second to toggle your map.

SPECIAL MANEUVERS

SLIDE DASH: Double-tap LEFT or RIGHT while on flat ground to perform a fast dodge.

ACTIVE PARRY: Hold UP and press B on the ground to brace for incoming physical attacks.

COMBAT & COUNTERPLAY

SWORD COMBOS

Use B button to unleash a devastating three-hit chain. The third swing is critical for destroying tougher heretics.

FERVOUR & THE PRAYER

Strike enemies to build Fervour. Press DOWN + B to spend it and unleash the Lorquiana, a piercing magical wave that deals heavy damage to all enemies in its path.

CHARGE ATTACK

Unlocked via the Mea Culpa Altar.Hold the B button and release to unleash a slash that hits with devastating purple fury, covering a larger area than any normal strike.

THE POGO BOUNCE

Strike enemies or hazards from directly above using your downward air thrust (Down + B in mid-air). This gives you an aerial leap, letting you cross large instant-death spike pits.

SUCCESSFUL PARRY CANCEL

When you parry, immediately press B to cancel recovery and execute an instant counterstrike!

BILE VESSEL HEALING

Double-tap DOWN while on flat ground to consume a Bile Vessel and recover HP.

FOES OF THE LAND

THE CRUCIFIED

Lost souls bound to massive wooden crosses. Watch their overhead frames! They hoist their cross back and swing it forward with giant reach. Parry their strike or jump over them.

ENRAGED PILGRIM

Wandering wastes in deep prayer, carrying heavy stone staves. They walk continuously and strike heretics with overhead swings. Attack them from behind or execute an Active Parry.

WHEELBROKEN

Spirits bound to spiked execution wheels, spinning uncontrollably across platforms. Standard attacks will bounce off their front. Jump over them or perform a mid-air Pogo Strike.

THE WARDEN (BOSS)

The Warden of the Silent Sorrow guards the first gate. He swings a massive stone slab. Slide-dash under him when he leaps into the air to avoid the shockwave, then strike his back while he recovers.

THE SACRED SECRETS

PRIE DIEU ALTARS

These stone shrines serve as checkpoints. Kneeling fully restores health and refuels empty Bile Vessels. However, doing so causes all defeated heretics in the level to respawn.

MEA CULPA ALTARS

Spent your collected Tears of Atonement here. Spent them to permanently double your sword damage or expand your capacity to carry additional Bile Vessels.

SECRET CODES

Ancient scrolls speak of a "Qo nai mei" ritual. It might have something to do with a certain button ritual.

VERTICAL ABYSS CONNECTIONS

Not all falls are lethal! Certain pits, such as the bottom of Level 10, will drop you into hidden sub-levels rather than killing you. Use these vertical paths to discover lost items and Tears!
