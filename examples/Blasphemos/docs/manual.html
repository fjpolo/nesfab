THE MIRACLE OF CVSTODIA

"For twisted are the paths of the Miracle."

In the land of Cvstodia, a foul curse known as The Miracle has fallen upon its inhabitants, turning their deepest guilt and piety into physical manifestations of agony and horror.

You are the Penitent One, the sole survivor of the brotherhood of the Silent Sorrow. Trapped in an endless cycle of death and rebirth, you must take up your sword, Mea Culpa, and find the source of your torment.

Only by completing your pilgrimage through the peaks and depths of Cvstodia can you hope to break the cycle and find true absolution.

4

CONTROLLING THE PENITENT ONE

D-PAD Move Left / Right
A BUTTON Jump
B BUTTON Attack / Pogo (mid-air)
UP Climb Ladders / Doors
START Pause Game
SELECT Hold for > 1 second to open/close the Map

SPECIAL MANEUVERS

DASH: Double-tap LEFT or RIGHT on the ground to perform a quick slide.

PARRY: Hold UP and press B while on the ground to brace for an attack.

5

COMBAT & PENANCE

MEA CULPA

Your sword, the Mea Culpa, is your only companion. Press B to strike. You can chain attacks into a three-hit combo by timing your presses.

THE POGO STRIKE

While jumping, press B again to perform a downward strike. If you hit an enemy or certain objects, you will bounce upward, allowing you to reach new heights.

HEALING

When your health is low, double-tap DOWN to consume a Bile Vessel. This will restore a portion of your health instantly.

NOTE: You can only carry a limited number of Bile Vessels. Refill them at any Prie Dieu (Checkpoint).
6

FOES OF CVSTODIA

THE CRUCIFIED

Sorrowful souls bound to their crosses. They will charge at you if you draw near.

ENRAGED PILGRIM

Armed with heavy staves, these pilgrims wander the wastes looking for heretics to crush.

WHEELBROKEN

Victims of the wheel, they spin uncontrollably across the ground. Jump over them or parry!

THE WARDEN

The Warden of the Silent Sorrow guards the first gate. He is massive and wields a heavy stone slab. Watch his movements and strike when he rests.

7

SECRETS

PRIE DIEU

These altars serve as checkpoints. Kneeling at a Prie Dieu will restore your health and refill your Bile Vessels, but be warned: all defeated enemies will respawn.

SECRET CODES

Ancient scrolls speak of a "Qo nai mei" ritual. It might have something to do with a certain button ritual.

MAP NAVIGATION

Cvstodia is vast. Look for ladders and hidden passages. Hold SELECT for more than a second to open or close your map. If a jump seems too far, try the POGO STRIKE on a flying enemy or background object.
